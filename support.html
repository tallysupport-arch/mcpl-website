Before you raise a ticket

Share the affected product, exact error message, when the issue started and what changed immediately before it occurred.

Use Critical only when a business-essential system is unavailable and there is no workable alternative.

Many software, configuration and user issues can be reviewed remotely. Hardware and site issues may require an on-site visit.
