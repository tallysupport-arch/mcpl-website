Our story

Built around real business needs, not just technology.

For more than two decades, MCPL has helped organisations make confident technology decisions and keep critical systems running smoothly.

What began with business software has grown into an end-to-end solutions practice covering TallyPrime, TSS, ERP and billing, desktops and servers, networking, cloud, cybersecurity, CCTV surveillance, AMC and facility management.

Our role is simple: understand the requirement, recommend the right-fit solution, implement it carefully and remain available when support is needed.
